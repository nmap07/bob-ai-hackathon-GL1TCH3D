# What to do now — a guide for you

Your reference number: **{case_reference}**
Contact: **{unit_name}**, {unit_contact}

## 1. You are not at fault
Someone may have made or shared a fake or edited picture, video or voice of you. This is a crime by them, not a mistake by you. You do not have to feel ashamed. Help is available.

## 2. Do this now
- **Do not delete anything.** Keep the messages, photos, videos, links and usernames.
- **Take screenshots** that show the date and time.
- **Do not pay any money.** Paying usually leads to more demands.
- **Do not send any more photos or videos.**
- Stop replying to the person. You can block them after you have saved everything.

## 3. Report it
- Online: **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal — https://cybercrime.gov.in**
- Phone: **1930** (cyber crime helpline)
- Or come to the cyber cell named above with your reference number.

## 4. Ask for the content to be removed
Use the "Report" option on the app or website where it was posted. Choose the option for fake, private or sexual content. Save the report number the platform gives you.
If intimate images of an adult are involved, ask the police whether a hash-based blocking service such as StopNCII can help you.

## 5. What the police will ask you for
- Your phone number and the phone numbers or accounts that contacted you
- Screenshots of messages, with dates
- Links to where the content was posted
- Any payment details (UPI ID, bank reference) if you paid or were asked to pay
- The phone or device where you received the content (please do not reset it)

## 6. What happens next
The cyber cell will contact you about the next steps. You can ask for an update at any time using your reference number.

## 7. If the person shown is under 18
Tell the police immediately. **Do not forward, copy or show the content to anyone**, even to prove what happened. The police will handle it.

## 8. Support
Talking to someone can help. Ask the cyber cell for a counselling or support helpline near you. If you feel unsafe or are thinking of harming yourself, call **112** now.
