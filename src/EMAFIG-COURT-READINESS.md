# EMAFIG Court-readiness notes

No automated system can guarantee admissibility. The design target is forensic reproducibility, integrity, validation, repeatability, and traceability.

India: the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 includes electronic-record provisions and a section 63(4)(c) certificate schedule with source/device and hash fields. The responsible person must complete any applicable statutory certificate. The system must never fabricate or sign that certificate.

NIST SP 800-86 describes digital-forensic practices including integrity checking with message digests, documentation, and forensic process controls, while stating it is not legal advice. ISO/IEC 27037 provides guidance for identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation of digital evidence.
